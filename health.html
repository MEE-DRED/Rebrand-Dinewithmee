Nutrition Support for Common Health Conditions

Sickle Cell

What it is

Sickle cell disease is an inherited blood disorder where red blood cells can become rigid and sickle-shaped, reducing oxygen flow.

Complications

  • Pain episodes and fatigue
  • Higher risk of infection
  • Anemia and reduced oxygen delivery

Nutrition guidance

  • Prioritize folate-rich greens and legumes
  • Hydrate throughout the day
  • Support immunity with colorful vegetables and fruit

Hypertension

What it is

Hypertension means blood pressure stays elevated over time, increasing strain on blood vessels and the heart.

Complications

  • Heart disease and stroke risk
  • Kidney stress
  • Long-term vascular damage

Nutrition guidance

  • Reduce salt-heavy processed foods
  • Emphasize potassium-rich vegetables and legumes
  • Choose grilled, steamed, or lightly sauteed meals

Diabetes

What it is

Diabetes affects blood sugar regulation and requires consistent nutrition patterns for better glycemic control.

Complications

  • Nerve and eye complications
  • Kidney strain over time
  • Greater cardiovascular risk

Nutrition guidance

  • Prioritize high-fiber and low-glycemic meals
  • Pair carbohydrates with protein sources
  • Keep portions steady across the day

Obesity

What it is

Obesity is excess body fat that can raise the risk of chronic disease and metabolic dysfunction.

Complications

  • Insulin resistance and type 2 diabetes risk
  • Joint stress and mobility limits
  • Higher blood pressure and lipid imbalance

Nutrition guidance

  • Build meals around vegetables, fiber, and protein
  • Limit sugar-dense beverages and snacks
  • Use consistent meal timing and hydration
