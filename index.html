Nutrition Knowledge

Nutrition and Health Guides

Quick insights first, with deeper guidance available only when you want it.

Diabetes

Learn blood sugar-friendly meal patterns using familiar African ingredients.

What it is: A condition where blood sugar regulation becomes impaired over time.

Key risks: Nerve damage, kidney strain, eye complications, and cardiovascular risk.

Nutrition tips: Prioritize fiber-rich meals, pair carbs with protein, and keep portions consistent.

Suggested meals: Waakye, Koshari, Githeri.

Hypertension

Support blood pressure with lower-sodium, nutrient-dense meal choices.

What it is: Persistently elevated blood pressure that increases vascular strain.

Key risks: Stroke risk, heart disease progression, and kidney complications.

Nutrition tips: Reduce processed salts, increase vegetables, and choose grilled over fried meals.

Suggested meals: Ful Medames, Tagine, Banku and Tilapia.

Obesity

Build satiety-focused meals that support sustainable weight management.

What it is: Excess body fat that can increase chronic disease and metabolic risk.

Key risks: Insulin resistance, joint stress, and long-term cardiovascular burden.

Nutrition tips: Increase protein and fiber, reduce sugary drinks, and keep meal timing regular.

Suggested meals: Pepper Soup, Ugali and Sukuma Wiki, Shiro.

Sickle Cell

Use nutrition to support blood health, recovery, and daily energy levels.

What it is: An inherited blood disorder affecting red blood cell shape and oxygen flow.

Key risks: Fatigue, recurrent pain episodes, and higher infection vulnerability.

Nutrition tips: Prioritize folate and iron sources, hydrate well, and maintain protein quality.

Suggested meals: Efo Riro, Molokhia, Ndole.

Maternal Nutrition

Trimester-aware nutrition that supports both maternal and fetal health outcomes.

What it is: Stage-based nourishment for pregnancy and postpartum recovery.

Key risks: Iron deficiency, poor fetal growth, and low maternal energy.

Nutrition tips: Focus on iron, folate, protein, hydration, and balanced meal spacing.

Suggested meals: Moi Moi, Isombe, Injera and Doro Wat.
